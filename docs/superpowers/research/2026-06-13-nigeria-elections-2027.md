## 4. 2027 Presidential / Party Landscape

The big story: the 2025 attempt at a single mega-opposition (ADC) **fractured into two rival vehicles**, splitting the anti-incumbent vote — structurally favoring Tinubu.

- **APC — Bola Tinubu** ✅ seeking re-election. Running mate **Shettima not confirmed** for 2027 ⚠️ (Muslim-Muslim ticket under pressure; a Christian VP swap is rumored).
- **ADC (coalition) — Atiku Abubakar** ✅ won the ADC primary 27–28 May 2026 (David Mark chairs the coalition). ⚠️ Result **contested** by runners-up Amaechi & Hayatu-Deen; ADC also split three ways factionally. Running mate TBD.
- **NDC — Peter Obi + Rabiu Kwankwaso** ✅ Obi left **Labour Party**, runs under the **Nigerian Democratic Congress**; Kwankwaso (ex-NNPP) is his running mate. *ADC and NDC are now competitors, not allies.*
- **Labour Party** — running its own candidate (Chibuzo Okereke) without Obi.
- **PDP** — hollowed out; two factions (Jonathan in absentia via Turaki faction; Sandy Onor via Wike faction). 6 governors defected to APC. Spoiler, not contender.
- **NNPP** — effectively absorbed into NDC via Kwankwaso.
- **Others:** Makinde (APM, also Oyo gov), Sowore (AAC), Adebayo (SDP), Olawepo-Hashim (Accord), Duke (PRP).

**Genuinely-opposition governors (Jan 2026 analysis):** essentially only **Makinde (Oyo)** and **Bala Mohammed (Bauchi)**; the other nominal-opposition governors have drifted toward accommodation with Tinubu. "De facto one-party state" is the live media framing.

## 5. INEC Processes for Diaspora

- **Diaspora voting: NOT available for 2027.** ⚠️ Constitutional constraint — voters must appear in person at their registered polling unit. A diaspora-voting bill + NiDRes App exist, but the required constitutional amendment (⅔ of state assemblies) has not passed in time.
- **CVR (Continuous Voter Registration):** ✅ runs in **periodic windows**, not permanently open. Diaspora can't register remotely.
- **BVAS** (biometric accreditation) + **IReV** (result-viewing portal): Electoral Act 2026 rewrote **§60(3)** to make **electronic transmission mandatory** and IReV data **admissible in tribunals** (was "directory only" under the 2022 Act). §47 gives BVAS first explicit statutory recognition. **Carve-out risk:** a "communication failure" claim lets the manual Form EC8A become primary — undefined, unverified, reproducing the 2023 upload-failure discretion.

## 6. Data & Boundary Sources (for the build)

### Boundaries — RECOMMENDED
- ❌ `temikeezy/nigeria-geojson-data` — **no geometry** (just name/LGA/ward lists + centroids). Useful for dropdowns, not maps.
- ✅ **geoBoundaries ADM1 (NGA)** — 37 features (36 states + "Abuja Federal Capital Territory"), `Polygon`/`MultiPolygon`. Properties: `shapeName`, **`shapeISO`** (ISO 3166-2, e.g. `NG-LA`, `NG-RI`, `NG-FC` — stable join key), `shapeID`. GRID3 source, CC BY 4.0.
  - Simplified GeoJSON (~828 KB, use this for web): `https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/raw/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/NGA/ADM1/geoBoundaries-NGA-ADM1_simplified.geojson`
  - Full (~2.3 MB): `...geoBoundaries-NGA-ADM1.geojson` · TopoJSON also available
  - Stable metadata API: `https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/NGA/ADM1/`
  - **Plan:** download once, convert to TopoJSON, store in `/public/data/nigeria-states.json` (mirrors the travel-map pipeline). Join election data on `shapeISO`.

### Live results / data feeds
**No public election-results API exists for Nigeria.** Options:
| Source | Access | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| INEC IReV | scrape on election day | Portal offline between cycles; no API |
| INEC main site | per-state PDFs | Not machine-readable |
| Stears / Dataphyte | web UI only | Reference, no API/export |
| `oladeeayo/NigeriaPresidentialElections2023` (GitHub) | CSV (7 KB) | Clean 37-row 2023 presidential results by state (APC/PDP/LP/NNPP/Others + turnout + centroids) — **best historical seed data** |

**Implication for the tracker:** for the foreseeable future this is a **manually-curated** tracker (calendar, candidates, per-state context, historical 2023 results), not a live results feed. Live results would require election-day scraping of IReV when it returns.
